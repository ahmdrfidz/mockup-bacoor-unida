Our Mission

Quality Christian education, rooted in Biblical principles.

Bacoor Unida Evangelical School helps every student discover their individual gifts and grow spiritually, socially, and intellectually — through the caring partnership of faculty, parents, and the Evangelical Christian Church.

Since our first class of eleven pupils, we've grown into a full Kinder-to-Secondary school still guided by the same purpose: forming students of strong character and genuine faith.

Our Story

Nearly five decades of growing, together.

From a single kindergarten class inside a church to a fully recognized Kinder–Secondary school — here's how we got here.

1977

A kindergarten class begins

Organized within Bacoor Unida Church as an act of child evangelism, first led by Mrs. Rosette Malinis-Infante.

1980

A season of pause

The school temporarily closes — but the vision doesn't end there.

1991

Reopened as Faith Christian Academy

With the encouragement of missionary-pastor Rev. Nephtali A. Rafael, classes resume under Ms. Nerla R. Sabino.

’92–’94

Government recognition begins

DepEd permits are granted for Pre-Elementary and Primary Course, and a School Board is formed to govern the growing institution.

’03–’06

Secondary course opens, year by year

First Year through Fourth Year are introduced in succession, culminating in full DepEd recognition of the Complete Secondary Course in January 2006.

Today

Bacoor Unida Evangelical School, Inc.

Now serving hundreds of students from Kinder to Fourth Year, guided by the same mission that started with eleven.
